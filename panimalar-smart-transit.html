01 • About The Project

Digital Visibility for Daily Campus Commutes

Re-engineering everyday college transportation through automated digital connectivity.

Panimalar Smart Transit was built as a dedicated college transportation management and live visibility platform serving students, drivers, and campus transport administrators within a unified ecosystem.

Instead of relying on guesswork, phone calls, or fragmented messaging groups to figure out whether a college shuttle is active or approaching, the platform provides structured real-time GPS visibility, availability indicators, and personalized boarding notifications.

Campus-Wide Coverage

Coordinates multiple shuttle paths connecting colleges across metropolitan pickup nodes.

Instant State Feedback

Live status badges update in sub-second intervals to reflect road delays and route changes.

02 • Problem Statement

Daily Campus Transit Visibility Challenges

Addressing commuter friction points through real-time telemetry and unified interfaces.

01

Uncertainty Around Bus Locations

Students waiting at pickup points previously had no digital way to confirm the current location of their college bus.

02

Lack of Availability & Active Status Visibility

No centralized indicator existed to confirm whether tracking was active, delayed, or temporarily offline on a given morning.

03

Manual Coordination Overhead

Transport coordinators and drivers faced repeated inquiries about arrival timing and boarding readiness.

04

Disconnected Multi-Role Workflows

Students, drivers, and transport administrators lacked a synchronized digital channel for pass validation and live transit status.

03 • Implemented Features

Core Smart Transit Platform Capabilities

Built around real-time GPS state synchronization, clear vehicle availability indicators, and role-specific dashboards.

Live College Bus Location Monitoring

Displays live updated vehicle coordinates streamed from the driver client to students and administrators in real time.

Active & Offline Availability Status

Clear status indicators distinguish active GPS tracking, minor traffic delays, and offline vehicle states at a glance.

Interactive Map Tracking View

Smooth animated map markers let users follow live movement across digital street map layers on both mobile and web.

Personal Boarding Stop Preference & Alerts

Students can choose their personal boarding preference in-app to receive proximity and arrival awareness alerts.

Estimated Arrival Visibility (ETA)

Computes live distance-based arrival countdowns relative to a student’s saved boarding preference.

Driver GPS Broadcast & Status Controls

Simple start/stop tracking controls let drivers stream geolocation updates, log boarding counts, and report operational delays.

Administrative Fleet Oversight

Centralized console giving transport staff live multi-vehicle visibility alongside digital student pass and registry tools.

Accessible Web & Mobile Experience

Responsive interface tailored for quick daily commute checks right from a student’s phone or desktop browser.

04 • Application Workflow

Real-Time Telemetry Flow Across All Roles

An end-to-end synchronized lifecycle from driver broadcast to student proximity alerts.

STEP 01
Driver Console

Driver Starts Live Tracking

At the start of a trip, the driver enables location broadcasting on the Driver interface after granting GPS permission.

STEP 02
Cloud Telemetry

Coordinates Sync to Firebase

Geolocation coordinates, accuracy, status flags, and timestamps stream continuously to Firebase Realtime Database.

STEP 03
Student App / Portal

Students View Live Position & Status

Student clients subscribe to live database listeners to update the interactive map marker and active status badge.

STEP 04
Proximity Engine

Arrival & Proximity Feedback

Distance calculation checks proximity to each user’s configured boarding point and triggers in-app ETA notifications.

STEP 05
Admin Dashboard

Fleet Overview & Coordination

Campus administrators oversee live bus availability, student pass approvals, and operational logs from a central console.
