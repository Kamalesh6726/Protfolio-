01 • About The Initiative

From Passive Recording to Active Public-Safety Intelligence

Transitioning urban surveillance from post-incident archival into proactive situational awareness.

Conventional closed-circuit television (CCTV) networks operate largely as passive recording infrastructure—storing footage for retrospective investigation after an incident has already occurred. Relying exclusively on continuous human operator vigilance across wide camera networks creates fatigue, blind spots, and critical response delays.

Singa Paarvai AI is proposed as an active public-safety intelligence layer. By combining automated video stream analysis, geographic incident localization, and structured alert dissemination, the initiative aims to help authorized authorities detect potential threats early, preserve structured incident records, and coordinate rapid intervention.

Conventional CCTV

Passive archival recording requiring continuous manual operator observation and post-incident review.

Singa Paarvai AI Approach

Automated computer vision analysis, real-time anomaly flagging, GPS coordinate tagging, and jurisdiction-routed alerts to authorized personnel.

02 • Problem Statement

Key Operational Gaps in Conventional Surveillance

Identifying why traditional camera deployments often fail to prevent escalation or accelerate emergency arrival.

01

Manual Monitoring Dependence

Security personnel face cognitive overload when watching dozens of live camera feeds simultaneously, increasing the likelihood of missed incidents.

02

Delayed Incident Identification

Without automated visual screening, suspicious behaviour, altercations, or threats often go unnoticed until reported manually by bystanders.

03

Emergency Communication Latency

Transferring location details, incident context, and jurisdiction routing through manual phone calls introduces critical response delays.

04

Multi-Location Scalability Gap

Expanding camera coverage across transport hubs, campuses, and urban corridors outpaces the availability of dedicated monitoring staff.

05

Evidence Collection & Management

Locating, verifying, and securing relevant video segments across distributed local recorders slows investigative follow-up.

03 • Proposed Solution

Proactive Intelligent Surveillance & Automated Dispatch Pipeline

Singa Paarvai AI integrates 360-degree smart visual capture with automated computer vision screening, GPS-based localization, police jurisdiction mapping, and encrypted cloud evidence retention into a cohesive public-safety workflow.

PILLAR 01
Continuous Visual Screening
Proposes automated real-time analysis of multi-angle camera streams to assist operators in spotting high-risk events immediately.
PILLAR 02
Location & Jurisdiction Context
Pairs each camera node with GPS metadata and smart jurisdiction boundaries so alerts reach the relevant local control centre without manual lookup.
PILLAR 03
Authorized Alert & Evidence Integrity
Generates structured notifications containing incident snapshots and metadata for authorized responders while preserving tamper-resistant cloud records.
