The MediLong Platform Architecture

As the Founder of MediLong, I led the complete product lifecycle from domain research and clinician interviews to full-stack engineering and cloud deployment. The platform addresses fragmented patient data, high clinic waiting times, and error-prone billing through six integrated modules:

Unified EHR & RBAC

Secure, centralized Electronic Health Records with granular role-based access for physicians, nurses, receptionists, and patients.

Intelligent Appointment Queue

Automated slot allocation and real-time patient queue optimization minimizing waiting room congestion across departments.

Digital Prescriptions & Pharmacy

Structured e-prescriptions with automatic dosage conflict checks and direct dispatch to hospital dispensary inventories.

Laboratory Diagnostics Telemetry

Direct laboratory panel ingestion with instant patient notifications and automated physician alert triggers on abnormal metrics.

Smart Billing & Insurance Claims

Transparent itemized billing pipelines integrated with standard diagnosis codes and instant exportable invoice summaries.

Hospital Capacity Analytics

Real-time executive telemetry tracking bed occupancy, daily patient throughput, and multi-department resource utilization.
